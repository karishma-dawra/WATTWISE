Weather-aware energy management

A 42°C day shouldn't wreck your month.

When a heatwave is forecast, cooling demand rises; on cold days heaters and geysers work harder. WATTWISE applies configurable, clearly stated assumptions to scale expected usage, then re-balances the units you have left across the remaining days.

Essentials such as the fridge and lights are reserved first. Comfort appliances get a protected minimum. The rest is shared by wattage and need — and we never claim exact demand from temperature alone.

What goes into every recommendation

  • Remaining monthly units and remaining billing days
  • Appliance wattage, quantity and usual daily hours
  • Essential load reserved before anything else
  • Tomorrow's forecast and the rest of the week
